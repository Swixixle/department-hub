# Reports about learning content

Design only. This is not an implemented report form or a claim of private server storage. It is separate from Ask's existing schedule, passage and interpretation corrections.

Use three categories, with a free-text explanation:

- Answer or rationale seems wrong.
- Question is unclear.
- Our local practice differs.

Do not use agreement totals as evidence that an answer is correct. Credentials and experience, if supplied, are self-reported context rather than verified qualifications or votes.

Retain the exact case/question version, answer and rationale shown, and the source references in force when the report was submitted. A later content update must not replace that snapshot. Keep the original report and append dated status changes, the reviewed source version and any resulting content revision.

Statuses are Received, Under review, Changed and Kept with source. Kept must identify the authority that settles the specific claim, its version/date and the relevant passage. A reviewer's opinion alone cannot settle a clinical correction. If no source settles it, leave it open. A difference in local practice is not automatically a correction to exam-targeted content; explain the scope of the cited authority.

Reports go privately to the project owner, not the department feed. Say who reads them before the person enters credentials or free text. Do not describe reports as anonymous. Shared implementation must enforce reviewer access on the server, including exports and retained snapshots; a hidden button or the current local profile selector does not provide that restriction. Current browser data is accessible to whoever controls that browser.

The reporting form, immutable content-version store, review inbox and authenticated access checks still need implementation in the learning-content system. This document does not add those features to Department Hub.
